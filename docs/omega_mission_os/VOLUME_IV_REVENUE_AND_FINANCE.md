# Volume IV — Revenue, Procurement, Customer, and Finance Engine

## Purpose

Convert legitimate opportunities into measured revenue through existing governed AIGC phases while preserving human approval, truthful claims, and financial traceability.

## Revenue lifecycle

`DISCOVER -> QUALIFY -> SCORE -> PACKAGE -> PRICE -> COMPLIANCE_REVIEW -> FIDELITY_REVIEW -> APPROVAL -> OUTREACH_OR_HANDOFF -> NEGOTIATION -> WORK_ACCEPTANCE -> DELIVERY -> INVOICE -> COLLECTION -> RETENTION`

## Opportunity score

Score expected net value using fit, buyer urgency, payment reliability, competition, delivery time, direct cost, compliance risk, evidence quality, strategic reuse, and probability of collection. Estimates must never be represented as committed or collected revenue.

## Offer architecture

Every offer includes a defined outcome, scope exclusions, inputs, deliverables, schedule, price, payment terms, revision boundary, acceptance test, data handling, intellectual-property position, and cancellation terms.

## Financial controls

Maintain separate values for pipeline, proposed, verbally committed, contracted, invoiced, collected, refunded, expense, tax reserve, and net cash. No connector may accept contracts, spend funds, issue invoices, or request payment without the required approval.

## Customer controls

Customer Satisfaction and quality scores remain internal forecasts until actual feedback exists. Claims, testimonials, and case studies require source evidence and permission.

## Initial revenue mission

For a CAD $2,000 monthly target with one to two operator hours per day, prefer a small number of fixed-scope, high-value, reusable services. Mission OS should prepare lead research, qualification, proposal packages, delivery plans, and follow-ups; the human operator approves external contact and contractual commitments.

## Acceptance criteria

Implementation requires machine-readable pipeline records, pricing and margin calculations, finance-ledger integration, compliance and quality gates, invoice-state tracking, collection evidence, and dashboards that never combine estimates with realized cash.
