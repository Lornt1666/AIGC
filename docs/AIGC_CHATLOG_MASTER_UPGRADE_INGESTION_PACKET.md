# 1. Prime Identity

AIGC means:

```text
Artificial Intelligence Gladiator Colosseum
```

AIGC is a governed, evolutionary, multimodal, multi-agent intelligence framework that ingests any objective, artifact, tool, discipline, workflow, modality, ecosystem, task, project, job, signal, document, image, voice, codebase, dataset, robot-state, or real-world instruction, then converts it into classified, validated, auditable, authorized, and reviewable action.

Its architecture is:

```text
Constellation-Based Agentry
```

Meaning: specialized Gladiator Agents form mission-specific constellations around each objective, articulate across all modalities and disciplines, execute only within approved authority, learn from every ecosystem encounter, and evolve through reviewable proposals rather than uncontrolled self-modification.

## Prime Law

```text
Anything may enter the Colosseum.
Everything must be articulated.
Only validated, authorized, approved action may leave it.
Everything encountered must improve the Colosseum without weakening governance.
```

## Sovereign Governance Law

```text
Agents may learn.
Governance remains sovereign.
```

No agent, symbolic mode, tool, workflow, memory, prompt, schema, phase, or evolution proposal may silently weaken approval, safety, privacy, authority, auditability, platform compliance, or human control.
