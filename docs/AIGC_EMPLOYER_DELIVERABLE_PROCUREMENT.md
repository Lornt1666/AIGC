## Prime Thesis

The final objective is not to publish the internal phase map.

The final objective is to prepare the strongest reviewable employer-facing deliverable possible:

```text
recognize the occupational discipline
  -> instantiate the right agent constellation
  -> apply best practices and emerging practices
  -> perform ology-based research synthesis
  -> coordinate data analytics and quality scoring
  -> perform reasoning and metacognitive review
  -> produce a submission-ready packet
  -> require human approval before submission
```

The phase map stays internal unless explicitly approved. The employer receives the polished work product, methodology summary, relevant audit summary, and evidence-backed value proposition.

## What This Stage Does

Employer Deliverable Procurement consumes the internal AIGC artifacts and creates:

- `data/employer_deliverables/aigc_employer_deliverable_procurement_packet.json`
- `reports/employer_deliverable_procurement_report.md`
- `data/audit/aigc_employer_deliverable_procurement_audit.jsonl`

It recognizes the occupational discipline, assigns agent roles, applies the ology stack, coordinates analytics, and prepares the deliverable sections that should be reviewed before submission.

## Occupational Discipline Recognition

The stage classifies project/job/task discipline across domains such as:

- software engineering;
- data analytics;
- business operations;
- security governance;
- construction trades;
- research synthesis;
- creative design and storytelling;
- generalist delivery when evidence is insufficient.

## Employer-Facing Boundary

The packet may include:

- methodology summary;
- audit summary;
- source citations when available;
- requirements and acceptance criteria;
- solution/work product plan;
- verification and fidelity review;
- risk and limitation notes;
- value proposition.

The packet must not automatically include:

- raw internal phase maps;
- private chain-of-thought style traces;
- credentials or secrets;
- unsupported payment guarantees;
- unreviewed external claims.

## Submission Boundary

This stage does not submit to an employer. It prepares the best possible packet for the Submission Approval Cockpit.
