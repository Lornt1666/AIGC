## Why this is the strongest fit

AIGC's bounded competition Project centers on governed AI-assisted opportunity assessment and a broader architecture intended to help people/businesses discover, assess, procure and execute economic work under explicit authority/evidence controls.

Relevant repository/business capabilities include:

```text
governed opportunity intake and assessment
work / Quest lifecycle concepts
human-reviewed AI decisions
employer/client and worker opportunity architecture
Revenue-Ω employer-funded placement/success economics
compute/API cost recovery
job/work creation and matching intent
proof-oriented agent execution
```

The intended economic outcome is not merely software automation for its own sake; it is increasing the ability to identify, qualify and execute economic opportunities and, where lawful, enable people to obtain paid work while the platform operates sustainably.

## Judge-facing impact thesis

Recommended bounded thesis:

> AIGC is designed to make economic opportunity more executable: AI helps structure and assess opportunities, governed controls prevent the model from self-authorizing consequential actions, human operators retain material authority, and evidence receipts make the resulting workflow auditable. The intended category impact is to reduce friction between an opportunity, a qualified human/business decision, and economically useful work.

Do not claim demonstrated job creation, employment placements, customer scale or revenue until real pilot/customer/payment evidence exists.
