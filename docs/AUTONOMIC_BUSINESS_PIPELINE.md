## Purpose

Phase 24 defines the full start-to-finished-project business cycle for AIGC.

The pipeline is designed as a transparent business operator system. It models neural-style microfunctions and multidisciplinary business agents, while keeping review, approval, compliance, and audit boundaries visible.

## Full lifecycle

1. **Find work** — discover approved sources and rank viable work by value, fit, safety, and legality.
2. **Ingest job** — convert the opportunity into a structured work order.
3. **Research job** — research customer, role, domain, constraints, context, and success criteria.
4. **Find best practices** — synthesize standards across business, technology, communication, compliance, and delivery.
5. **Deep dive into work** — map hidden requirements, assumptions, risks, proof artifacts, and implementation plan.
6. **Instantiate learning measures** — define quality deltas, uncertainty reduction, customer-fit score, and outcome learning.
7. **Design appropriate agents** — choose specialist agents and bind each to role, inputs, outputs, checks, and limits.
8. **Use agents** — prepare internal drafts, implementation plans, reviews, and audit-ready handoffs.
9. **Governed handoff** — move only visible, approved, non-expired artifacts into handoff readiness.
10. **Evolve structure** — feed outcomes into memory, metrics, and next-cycle improvement patches.

## Neural-style microcycle

Phase 24 represents the smallest functional loops as deterministic control modules:

- sensory intake;
- attention;
- working memory;
- valuation;
- planning;
- motor gate;
- feedback.

The result is not a hidden humanoid identity. It is a transparent operator architecture for business work.

## Business agent team

Phase 24 instantiates the following internal agents:

- Opportunity Scout;
- Job Intake Analyst;
- Research Strategist;
- Best-Practice Synthesist;
- Technical Deep-Dive Agent;
- Learning Measures Agent;
- Business Operator;
- Compliance and Ethics Officer;
- Execution Coordinator.

Each agent is multidisciplinary, governed, and bound to reviewable artifacts.
