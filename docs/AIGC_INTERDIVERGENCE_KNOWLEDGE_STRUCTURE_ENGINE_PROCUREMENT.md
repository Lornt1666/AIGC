# 1. Prime Thesis

AIGC must become multi-structural, not merely multi-disciplinary.

AIGC Interdivergence means:

```text
Split one idea into many knowledge structures.
Let each structure diverge into its own specialized lens.
Interconnect the lenses.
Recompose the strongest synthesis into a multimodal governed output packet.
```

Formal definition:

```text
Interdivergence = Divergence + Interconnection + Recomposition
```

AIGC does not merely know things.

```text
AIGC dissects reality into interoperable knowledge structures,
then recomposes them into governed action.
```

Purpose: explain cause, effect, root cause, intervention, and consequence.

Engine:

```text
CausalGraphEngine
```

Questions:


# 6. Safety Invariants

1. Interdivergence is analysis, not authority.
2. Interdivergence may reveal action paths, but it cannot execute them.
3. Legal/authority structures are routing aids, not legal advice.
4. Economic structures are planning aids, not financial guarantees.
5. Mathematical structures are formalizations, not proof of reality unless evidence supports them.
6. External actions remain blocked until approved.
7. Physical actions remain blocked until simulation, safety case, device permission, emergency stop path, and approval exist.
8. High-upside unsafe paths must become review packets or block records.
9. Every significant packet must be ledger-ready.
10. Every completed run should emit an evolution signal.
