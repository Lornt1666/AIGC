# Epoch I Research Alignment

AEC-001 and its supporting controls are aligned conceptually with recognized engineering guidance while making no certification claim.

## Normative and informative references

- NIST SP 800-218 Secure Software Development Framework: governance, protected development environments, verification, vulnerability response, and evidence-backed secure-development practices.
- NIST minimum software verification guidance: threat modelling, automated tests, static analysis, secret detection, structural testing, fuzzing where applicable, and dependency review.
- JSON Schema Draft 2020-12: strict machine-readable contracts and unknown-property rejection.
- W3C WCAG 2.2: accessibility requirements for future user-facing governance and assurance interfaces.
- SLSA provenance concepts: revision, build, artifact, and evidence provenance.
- NIST OSCAL concepts: machine-readable control catalogs, profiles, implementations, and assessment results as a future interoperability path.

## Application to Epoch I

- Constitutional articles remain technology-neutral.
- Supporting schemas are deterministic and versioned.
- Amendments require compatibility, migration, rollback, review, and ratification.
- Waivers are scoped, expiring, revocable, and fail closed.
- Governance receipts bind claims to revisions and evidence.
- CI validates the Constitution and governance registry before later stages.
- Accessibility and plain-language obligations remain constitutional even before a portal exists.

These sources guide architecture and controls. Compliance or certification requires a separate formal assessment and operational evidence.
