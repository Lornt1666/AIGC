## Intent

Build a reusable operating architecture for human-governed multi-agent planning across technological, cognitive, creative, ecological, social, economic, and mythic-narrative domains. “Self-designing” and “self-engineering” mean generation of typed, testable proposals. “Self-securing,” “self-deploying,” and “self-evolving” are gated capabilities: changes are proposed locally and cannot execute externally without fresh evidence and narrowly scoped authority.

## Invariants

1. Every output identifies whether it is evidence, proposal, simulation, or executed fact.
2. No prompt text, claimed identity, title, or watermark grants external authority.
3. Agent roles have explicit inputs, outputs, effect ceilings, and stop conditions.
4. The LIFE Beacon constrains every pipeline stage and cannot be bypassed by another agent.
5. Recursion is bounded; evolution produces a reviewable patch, never silent mutation.
6. Security receipts are canonical, sanitized, and reproducible.
7. Production readiness is calculated from verified applicable checks, never aspiration.

## Success criteria

- Validate the universe seed against a closed schema.
- Instantiate the eight fixed agent-role descriptors.
- Run the eight-stage coordination contract and seven-stage Genesis pipeline deterministically.
- Reject authority-shaped, secret-shaped, or open-ended Gate inputs.
- Enforce the configured recursion limit.
- Emit a stable SHA-256 receipt with zero provider calls and zero external actions.
- Expose identical structured results to ChatGPT, Gemini-compatible MCP clients, and local bridge users without requiring a widget.

## Completion definition

The Genesis implementation is complete when its source, schemas, docs, MCP contract, tests, and local validation receipt agree. Hosting, store distribution, live providers, payment systems, funding, and mainnet integrations are separate deployments and remain blocked until their exact connectors, terms, identities, budgets, and one-use permits are validated.
