## Quantum-Aware Boundary

Quantum-aware means deterministic uncertainty-aware quality-state modeling. Phase 23 preserves quality score, uncertainty score, collapse conditions, blocked actions, and required next actions without claiming literal quantum cognition.

## Safety Boundary
