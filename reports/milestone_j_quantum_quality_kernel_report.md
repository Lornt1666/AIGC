## Quantum-Aware Boundary

Quantum-aware means deterministic uncertainty-aware quality-state modeling. It does not mean literal quantum cognition, hidden capability claims, or probabilistic permission to act.

Phase 22 models:

- quality score
- uncertainty score
- quality state
- unresolved risk flags
- collapse conditions before external action
- ology-based self-audit
- internal agent-panel findings
- improvement directives

## Proto-Omni Cycle

Every quality kernel record contains:

- Perfect Draft v1: the initial internal quality synthesis.
- Ontological Self-Audit: structured critique across the ology matrix.
- Ascended Output v2: the improved internal readiness state and directives.

## Safety Boundary

Phase 22 performs no external action. It does not approve actions, contact customers, submit work, accept contracts, request payment, use credentials, or bypass Phase 5.

Any reviewed artifact remains blocked from external execution until legal, ethical, security, compliance, and scoped human approval gates are satisfied.
