## 1. Creative intent

A grief-stricken mother climbs an abandoned radio tower to send one final signal and receives what may be a transmission from her lost daughter. The tone moves from isolation and mechanical decay to warmth, uncanny recognition, and finally transcendent hope.

Visual grammar:
- present day: cold cyan shadows, rust, wind, long lenses, restrained movement;
- flashback: warm amber light, soft diffusion, intimate framing;
- signal event: pale volumetric light, controlled bloom, minimal spectacle;
- final image: emotional release rather than literal explanation.

## 5. Continuity bible

Mara:
- late 30s to mid 40s;
- weathered but composed;
- dark practical coat, muted scarf, gloves, climbing harness;
- no visible futuristic technology;
- emotional progression: resolve → recognition → grief → hope.

Lia:
- 7–9 years old;
- warm, playful, natural performance;
- simple timeless clothing;
- must not visually resemble a real child without written guardian consent.

Tower and transmitter:
- isolated, non-branded, rusted but structurally plausible;
- transmitter is analog, weather-beaten, with tactile knobs and dim lamps;
- no inconsistent dial layout across shots.

Beam:
- pale white with faint cyan edge;
- volumetric, soft, never weapon-like;
- should illuminate atmosphere and ground rather than appear as a flat laser.

## 6. Audio cue sheet

A01 — plains wind, low-frequency rumble, metal strain, distant cable resonance.
A02 — ladder contact, clothing movement, controlled breathing.
A03 — transmitter relay click, transformer hum, electrical buzz.
A04 — radio static layers: broadband hiss, crackle, narrowband sweep.
A05 — Morse motif: client must approve exact encoded meaning before lock.
A06 — flashback room tone: soft home ambience, child laughter, restrained piano.
A07 — voice in static: intelligible but not clean; center channel with filtered reflections.
A08 — sky event: low harmonic choir/radio hybrid, no recognizable copyrighted sample.
A09 — final score: hopeful strings, restrained percussion, radio chimes.
A10 — final mix targets: dialogue intelligible, no clipping, consistent loudness, clean fade.

## 7. Production method

Preferred closure-first method:
- create still keyframes for every shot;
- lock character and environment references;
- assemble a timed animatic with temp voices and sound;
- generate or film only approved shots;
- use short shot durations to hide generation drift;
- stabilize, upscale, interpolate only after picture selection;
- edit picture before final music and sound design;
- perform one technical QC pass and one emotional/story pass.
