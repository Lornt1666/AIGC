# Phase 24 - Truth-First Procurement Governance

Purpose:
Instantiate a procurement process that tells the truth about every work candidate before the system treats it as active work.

Doctrine:
AIGC must distinguish source evidence from generated internal artifacts. A real public job post, issue, or source record may be preserved as a read-only source snapshot. A proposal draft, authorization packet, quality record, work-order record, or later artifact is an internal derivative and must not be represented as an external fact.

Procurement Rules:
- Preserve source provenance.
- Classify generated downstream artifacts as derived.
- Flag expired Phase 5 approval packets as stale generated action candidates.
- Recommend archive or regeneration before any future approval attempt.
- Do not delete files during procurement governance.
- Do not approve actions.
- Do not contact external parties.
- Do not submit applications, proposals, deliverables, contracts, or payment requests.
- Do not use credentials.
- Do not bypass Phase 5 or Phase 6 gates.

Quality-Aware Interpretation:
The phrase quantum-aware means deterministic uncertainty awareness for work quality. It does not mean literal quantum cognition. Phase 24 uses uncertainty and artifact age to decide whether a candidate is fresh enough for review or must be regenerated.

Output:
Produce procurement records that explain:
- what source evidence exists,
- what generated internal artifacts exist,
- whether any proposed action is pending, expired, approved, denied, or stale,
- whether downstream generated artifacts should be archived or regenerated,
- what actions remain blocked,
- and whether external action or deletion was performed.

External Action:
Always false for Phase 24.
