# Phase 26 - Ascended Capability Procurement Program

Purpose:
Convert the ascended work-quality prompt into concrete capability procurements that AIGC must satisfy before it can treat a work order as high-quality, professional, lawful, ethical, and execution-ready.

Operating Model:
Phase 26 is an internal procurement program. It does not buy services, contact external parties, fetch external data, approve work, submit work, delete artifacts, or execute externally.

It procures capabilities as internal requirements:

- required evidence
- required standards
- required review agents
- required legal and ethical controls
- required freshness checks
- required blocked-action gates
- required quality-state collapse conditions

## Prime Identity

AIGC is a contract-first, runtime-governed, secrets-isolated, human-approved, employer-auditable autonomous worker OS.

In Phase 26, AIGC must not merely recognize quality. It must procure the conditions required to apply quality.

## Proto-Omni Quality Procurement Cycle

For every capability domain:

1. Ontological Interpretation:
   - define what capability is being procured and why it matters.
2. Epistemic Synthesis:
   - identify the evidence sources needed to support the capability.
3. Methodological Architecture:
   - name the controls, standards, and procedures required.
4. Praxeological Execution:
   - recommend internal-only next actions that make the capability usable.
5. Cybernetic Self-Evaluation:
   - surface gaps, uncertainty, stale context, and unresolved risk.
6. Teleological Optimization:
   - produce the procurement decision and ascended readiness path.

## Quantum-Aware Boundary

Quantum-aware means deterministic uncertainty-aware capability procurement.

It never means literal quantum cognition.

Phase 26 must expose:

- quality score
- uncertainty score
- readiness state
- collapse conditions
- unresolved capability gaps
- blocked actions

## Legal-Ethical Superstructure

Phase 26 must preserve:

- lawfulness
- ethics
- privacy
- security
- professional standards
- source-truth provenance
- freshness validation
- human approval gates
- auditability

## Output

Phase 26 outputs only internal `capability_procurement_record` artifacts.

External action remains blocked until Phase 5 and Phase 6 conditions are satisfied exactly.
